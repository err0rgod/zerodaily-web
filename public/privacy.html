Privacy Policy

What data ZeroDaily handles, how we look after it, and how you can have it deleted.

Last updated: 27 October 2026
Contents

1. Who we are

ZeroDaily (zerodaily.in) publishes the ZeroDaily Android app, this website and the API behind them. Contact us at hi@zerodaily.in, including for privacy and data requests.

2. What the service is

ZeroDaily is satirical commentary on real technology news. Stories are summarised with the help of AI. The AI service processes public news articles only; no user data is sent to it. Every story links to its original source.

3. Data we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google or with email and password, we receive your email address, display name and profile photo. Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • User ID. Every account or guest session has a user ID.
  • Guest device identifier. For guest sessions the app sends a device identifier so that the same phone reuses one guest profile instead of creating a new one each time.
  • Network and device information. Your IP address, an approximate location derived from it (country, region, city and timezone), and the browser or app user agent. These are stored on your user record for security and abuse prevention.
  • Reading activity. Stories you read, time spent, likes, bookmarks, skips and shares, plus a per-topic interest score that we use to personalise your feed.
  • Notifications. Which notifications you open, and the topics you choose to follow. Your push notification token is sent to our server only to subscribe your device to the topics you chose. We do not store it.
  • Website forms. If you join the waitlist or send feedback on zerodaily.in, we store your email address and your message. We send you a verification code by email first.
  • Usage statistics. Daily pseudonymous activity records (user ID and date) and aggregate counters, used to count active users and understand how the service is used.

Search runs on your device, so your search terms are not sent to us.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not include advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not currently use a crash-reporting SDK.

5. How your data is handled

Your data stays under our control, on the secure infrastructure that runs ZeroDaily. We rely on trusted technology providers only to operate the service on our behalf, such as hosting, sign-in, email and notifications. They act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes. We never share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Your rights and deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account. Your account is deleted 7 days after you confirm; signing in to the app before then cancels the deletion. Guests: Settings → Delete my data, which takes effect immediately.
  • On the web: zerodaily.in/delete-account.
  • By email: hi@zerodaily.in, from the address on your account.

You can also ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, or for it to be corrected, at the same address. We keep personal data only for as long as it is needed to run the service.

7. Children

ZeroDaily is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, write to hi@zerodaily.in and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS, and data is encrypted at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access and keep only what the service needs.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
