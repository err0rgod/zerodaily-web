Privacy Policy & Data Governance

1. Introduction & Privacy Philosophy

ZeroDaily (“we”, “our”, or “the Service”) is an independent technology news intelligence service delivering short, no-spin summaries across seven editorial desks (Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Robotics, Defense & Aerospace, Silicon, and Financial Markets).

Our business model is built on concise editorial curation, not surveillance capitalism. We collect only the minimum data required to authenticate you, synchronize your saved stories across devices, and deliver breaking news alerts you explicitly request. We do not construct advertising dossiers, sell behavioral profiles, or license data to brokers.

2. Information We Collect & How It Is Handled

Depending on whether you browse as an anonymous guest or sign in with an account, we process the following categories of data:

A. Authentication & Account Identity

You may use ZeroDaily without creating an account (Anonymous Guest Mode). When you choose to create an account or sign in via Google Sign-In or Firebase Email Authentication, Google Firebase processes the authentication exchange. We receive and store:

  • Unique User Identifier (Firebase UID): An alphanumeric token generated by Firebase to identify your account.
  • Email Address: Used for account recovery, authentication confirmation, and compliance communications.
  • Display Name & Profile Photo URL: Provided by your Google account profile to personalize your in-app settings header.
Password Security: ZeroDaily servers never see, handle, or store your passwords. All credential verification is executed directly by Google Firebase Auth over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.

B. Push Notification Architecture (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

ZeroDaily distributes breaking news briefings and editorial alerts using Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). We have specifically architected our notification delivery to minimize personal data footprint:

  • Topic-Based Subscriptions: Instead of indexing individual personal device tokens in a centralized user tracking database, your client app subscribes directly to broadcast topic channels (e.g., topic_breaking_all, topic_cybersec, topic_ai).
  • Zero Device Token Database: ZeroDaily backend servers do not maintain an inventory of APNs/FCM hardware tokens linked to personal identities.
  • Granular Category Control: You can enable or disable individual categories or silence all push notifications at any time via the in-app Settings modal or your Android system settings (Android 13+ POST_NOTIFICATIONS runtime permission).

C. Reading Telemetry & On-Device Personalization

To prevent showing you stories you have already completed, calculate read counts, and calibrate your editorial category affinity, the mobile app records pseudonymous interaction events:

  • Dwell Time Threshold (≥ 2.0s): When you view an article card for 2.0 seconds or longer, an on-device dwell event is recorded in local storage (@zerodaily_read_history via AsyncStorage). Stories skimmed under 2.0s are not cataloged as read.
  • Interaction Signals: Events including bookmarking, sharing, expanding a full roast punchline, and likes are logged pseudonymously to tune feed anti-clumping algorithms.
  • Local Storage & 30-Day Retention: Reading history and category affinity scores (algo_weights) reside primarily in your physical device's local storage and automatically roll off after 30 days.
  • Notification CTR Diagnostics: When tapping a breaking news push alert, an anonymous click-through signal measures dwell duration and swipe count until the app is backgrounded, evaluating whether the editorial summary matched reader expectations.

3. How We Use and Share Information

We process your data exclusively for the following functional purposes:

  • Delivering, rendering, and caching editorial briefings across our seven coverage desks.
  • Synchronizing saved bookmarks across multiple devices when signed into your account.
  • Broadcasting breaking alerts matching your opted-in notification categories.
  • Preventing repetitive or already-read briefings from cluttering your daily feed.
  • Protecting the integrity, rate limits, and security of our backend APIs against scraping and malicious automated traffic.
Absolute Prohibition on Data Brokerage: ZeroDaily does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data, email address, reading telemetry, or device identifiers to any data broker, advertising network, or commercial third party under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure & Service Providers

We rely on trusted enterprise cloud infrastructure providers to operate the service securely:

  • Google Cloud Platform & Firebase (USA): Provides Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (for synced user bookmarks and profiles), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications. All interactions are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA / Global Anycast): Provides edge content delivery, DDoS mitigation, DNS resolution, and security headers for zerodaily.in and API gateways.
  • GitHub (Microsoft): Hosts open-source client repositories and releases for public transparency.

5. Data Retention, Account Deletion & Grace Period

We maintain strict data minimization principles:

  • In-App Self-Serve Deletion: You can delete your account at any time by opening the mobile app, navigating to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  • Web-Based Deletion Portal: In compliance with the Google Play 2024 User Data Policy, you may request account deletion directly from the web without having the app installed by visiting our dedicated Account Deletion Request Portal.
  • 24-Hour Recovery Grace Period: When an account deletion is initiated, the account status is immediately marked is_pending_deletion: true, active session tokens are revoked, and push notification topics are unsubscribed. A 24-hour grace window begins. If you change your mind, logging back into the app within 24 hours cancels the deletion request.
  • Permanent Purge: Once the 24-hour grace period elapses, your user profile, Google OAuth linkage, cloud bookmarks, reading telemetry, and algorithmic weights are permanently expunged from our databases.
  • Local Cache Removal: Because on-device reading logs are stored locally on your device in Android AsyncStorage, you can erase them immediately by clearing the app data in Android Settings → Apps → ZeroDaily → Storage → Clear Data or by uninstalling the application.

6. Your Legal Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Indian DPDP)

Depending on your geographical jurisdiction, you are entitled to specific statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • European Economic Area & UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): Under Articles 15 through 22, you possess the right to access your personal data, rectify inaccuracies, request erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”), restrict or object to processing, and request portability of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information is collected, disclosed, or retained; the right to delete your personal information; the right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights; and the right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal data (we do not sell or share your data).
  • Indian Residents (Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 - DPDP): You possess the right to access a summary of personal data processed, the right to correction and erasure of personal data, the right to nominate an individual in the event of death or incapacity, and the right to readily accessible grievance redressal.

To exercise any statutory right, please submit a request via our Web Deletion Portal or email our Data Protection Officer at privacy@zerodaily.in. We respond to all verified requests within thirty (30) calendar days.

7. Security Measures & Encryption Standards

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • All client-to-server and cloud-to-cloud communications are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / TLS 1.2).
  • Data stored in Google Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Web pages enforce strict security headers: X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin.

8. Children's Privacy

ZeroDaily is intended for general tech-interested audiences and professionals. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area). If we learn that a child under the relevant age has provided personal data without verified parental consent, we will immediately purge that information from our servers.
