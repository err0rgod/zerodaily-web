Technical Disclosures & Local Device Cache

ZeroDaily adheres strictly to the Google Play 2024 User Data Policy regarding external account deletion and data retention transparency:

1. What Cloud Data is Permanently Purged

When the 24-hour grace period expires, our automated backend processes permanently delete:

  • Your Firebase User Account and Google OAuth identity linkage.
  • Your display name, email address, and profile photo reference.
  • Your synchronized cloud bookmarks and saved article collections.
  • Your historical category affinity weights (algo_weights) and personalization vectors.

2. On-Device Local Cache Removal Instructions

ZeroDaily utilizes an on-device local storage engine (AsyncStorage) to track read history (@zerodaily_read_history) to prevent duplicate card rendering. This local cache resides on your physical smartphone and is not accessible remotely by our servers.

To purge local device data immediately:
Open your phone's Settings → Apps → ZeroDaily → Storage → Clear Data, or uninstall the application. This immediately purges all on-device offline caches and tokens.

3. Security Audit Logs

Pseudonymized HTTP gateway logs (containing IP address and request timestamp) are retained by Cloudflare and edge infrastructure for up to thirty (30) days solely for the legitimate interest of detecting distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, brute-force exploits, and maintaining infrastructure availability. These logs are never linked to personal profiles and are automatically rotated.

4. Direct Offline Fallback

If you experience connectivity issues with this web form or require manual processing by our Data Protection Officer, you may initiate your request directly by emailing privacy@zerodaily.in from the email account registered with ZeroDaily.
