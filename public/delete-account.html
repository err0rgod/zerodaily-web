Use a different email or request a new code

Deletion requested

What happens next

Your account is scheduled for deletion 24 hours from the moment you confirm. If you sign in to the app before then, the deletion is cancelled. After that, it is removed automatically, normally within 2 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, including email, display name and profile photo reference.
  • Your reading activity, bookmarks and per-topic interest scores.
  • Your topic preferences.
  • Your waitlist entry, if you joined with this email.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Database backups, for up to 35 days.
  • Server logs, for 14 days.
  • Anonymous statistics that cannot be tied back to you.

Guests

If you use ZeroDaily without an account, you can delete your data instantly in the app: Settings → Delete my data. Guest profiles are also deleted automatically 30 days after last use.
