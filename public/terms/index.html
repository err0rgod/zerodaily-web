2. Nature of Service & Editorial Curation

ZeroDaily is an editorial technology news intelligence platform. We monitor corporate press releases, engineering post-mortems, regulatory filings, research papers, and technology reporting, synthesizing each story into an objective 60-word briefing paired with a biting editorial punchline (“roast”).

Our coverage spans seven dedicated editorial desks:

  • Cybersecurity: Zero-day exploits, threat actor campaigns, breach disclosures, and cryptographic vulnerabilities.
  • Artificial Intelligence: Foundation model benchmarks, compute economics, research breakthroughs, and algorithmic developments.
  • Software Engineering: Infrastructure architectures, compiler innovations, runtime ecosystems, and developer tooling.
  • Robotics & Automation: Embodied intelligence, spatial computing, autonomous vehicles, and industrial automation.
  • Defense & Aerospace: Orbital deployments, satellite constellations, avionics, and national security technologies.
  • Silicon & Hardware: Fabrication nodes, semiconductor foundries, wafer packaging, and processor architectures.
  • Finance & Tech Economics: Venture capital flows, IPO disclosures, antitrust enforcement, and tech enterprise earnings.
